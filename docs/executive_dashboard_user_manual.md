# Executive Dashboard User Manual

## Select a period

Choose the period type in the filter, then select a financial year, quarter/half-year part, month or custom start and end dates when those controls appear. Select **Apply** to refresh all cards, charts and export links. The financial year begins in April and ends in March. A selected financial year can be shown as the full year, Q1-Q4 or H1-H2. The comparison uses the immediately previous equivalent period.

## Read the financial indicators

- **Revenue** is taxable invoice value excluding GST and follows invoice date.
- **Amount received** follows the actual receipt date. It is cash collection, not additional revenue.
- **Gross salary** is the finalized current payroll gross amount. Employee deductions are shown for information and already sit within gross salary.
- **Employer contributions** are additional payroll cost. **Total salary cost** is gross salary plus employer contributions.
- **Net salary payable** is the payroll net amount; it is not a second expense. **Salary paid** is recorded cash by payment date.
- **Employee reimbursements** and company expenses follow the application report's existing approved/cancellation rules.
- **Outstanding balances** are point-in-time figures as of the selected period end (capped at today). Salary payable is finalized net salary less payments recorded by that date.
- Profit, margin and total expense cards use the mixed accrual management P&L basis. They are not statutory accounts.

Cards show the prior equivalent value, absolute difference and percentage change. Where the prior period is zero, the change is shown as **New** or **n/a**.

## Read the charts and drill down

Charts use actual stored data for the selected months. The payroll component chart separates gross, deductions, net and employer contributions. Revenue is compared with gross salary plus employer contributions for employee cost analysis. The cash chart uses recorded customer receipts and recorded payments; other receipt sources are not inferred. Expense composition separates salary cost, company expenses and approved reimbursements.

Select a KPI card or chart title to open the corresponding detailed report. Use report navigation for customer, vendor, employee, department, invoice and transaction-level analysis. Existing report filters and totals remain authoritative for detailed investigation.

## Export

- **Download selected-period workbook** creates an Excel workbook with 11 sheets based on existing reports. Each sheet uses the same selected date period.
- **Download selected-period P&L PDF** exports the management P&L for that period. Other detailed reports can be exported to PDF or Excel from their report pages.

Check the selected period and accounting basis before sharing an export.
